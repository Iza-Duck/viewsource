THE BLACK CAT
One stormy night, a black cat was walking down a back alley. While he was looking for shelter, he saw a big blue volt of lightning come his way. WHAM He was struck by lightning. He panicked,squinted his eyes, but quickly realized he was okay. When he opened his eyes back up, he realize he was somewhere new. Not in the back alleyway, he was in the country. Cows were looking back at him. One of them mooed and startled the poor cat again. Now it's up to you. Where will the cat teleport next?
